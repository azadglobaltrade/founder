COLOPHON

How to use this field guide.

Charlie Munger's idea: carry a latticework of models in your head and reach for the right one. This site gives you 963 of them — named, numbered, illustrated and wired to neighbours.

Start in the Library Or follow the Founder Track Or explore the Lattice

1 · Browse

Filter by discipline, search by problem. Every plate has a number (001–963), a tight definition, a concrete scenario and an LLM prompt. Try the Library search or the Lattice map search.

2 · Study

Tap Mark studied when an idea clicks. Progress persists in your browser across all four pages — build your own lattice, one plate at a time.

3 · Apply

Copy the prompt, paste into ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini with your real problem attached. Ask for second-order effects and disconfirmation. The Founder Track shows how: one real business problem per subject.

Method & credits

Plates

~70 famous models are hand-written (inversion, moats, Pareto, entropy, OODA, Lindy…). 24 founder plates cover your AGT capital × decisions × leverage curriculum. The remainder deterministically fill each discipline to 963 so every filter is complete.

Connections

Each plate links to ~5 neighbours: same-discipline siblings plus bridges to related disciplines and one long-range jump — 2,566 edges total, shared by the Library, the plate modal and the Lattice map.

Design

Set in Fraunces & Space Mono on warm paper #F3EFE4 · ink #211D14 · ember #C65A2E · verdigris #2E7F74. A loving study-copy inspired by latticework.page, built for learning.
