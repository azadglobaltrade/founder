STUDY MAP · MILITARY WARFARE STRATEGIES

How limited means serve political ends.

Military strategy studies how states, commanders and organizations use limited resources, information, time, alliances and force to achieve political objectives. This map organizes the major concepts as a study curriculum — historical and analytical, not instructions for violence. Framework after Echevarria's Military Strategy: A Very Short Introduction.

0 studied

1 · Hierarchy of strategy

Grand strategy

National objectives, diplomacy, economics, alliances and military power.

Military strategy

How military power contributes to political objectives.

Operational art

Connect objectives, resources, time and multiple operations.

Tactics

How forces conduct particular engagements and immediate actions.

Winning an engagement need not win the campaign; winning the campaign need not achieve the political objective.

4 · Forms, domains, thinkers, principles

GroupWhat to study
Forms of warfareConventional, asymmetric, guerrilla, insurgency, counterinsurgency, proxy, hybrid, coalition, expeditionary, urban.
DomainsMaritime and sea control; air superiority; space services; cyber resilience; information and intelligence; electronic warfare; nuclear deterrence; economic and industrial endurance.
ThinkersSun Tzu, Clausewitz, Jomini, Liddell Hart, Mahan, Corbett, Boyd (OODA), Schelling.
PrinciplesObjective, concentration, economy of force, maneuver, initiative, security, surprise, unity of effort, simplicity, logistics, adaptation, political purpose.

Study path: Sun Tzu + Clausewitz, historical campaigns with maps, deterrence and logistics, then game theory and systems analysis. Caution: business competition is not warfare — translate concentration, intelligence, logistics and alliances ethically; never copy deception or coercion literally.
